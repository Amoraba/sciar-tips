For restaurant owners

Card tips, paid out to your team every Monday.

Sciar Tips connects to your card terminal and pays the week's card tips to your staff, under the split rule you choose.

Join the alpha: email us

Three things that are true today

  1. You set the rule, we pay it out every Monday. Card tips from your terminal go into one weekly pool. You set the split rule in the owner dashboard, and Sciar pays each person straight to their bank account on Monday.[1]
  2. In our pilot, the card tips went to staff, to within rounding. Over a 12-week pilot at one Dublin restaurant, €31,754.13 of card tips came in and €31,754.09 was paid out to staff. The €0.04 gap is rounding each payout to the cent.[2]
  3. Your staff can see how their own pay was worked out. In this alpha, each person sees their own hours, the week's tip pool and their share, on their phone, and can raise a concern. They never see a colleague's pay.[3]
